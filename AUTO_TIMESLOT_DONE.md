## What I Created

**Automatic time slot generator** - Create all your weekly time slots with ONE CLICK! ⚡

## 🎯 Features

### Default Settings (Perfect for your request!):
- ✅ **Monday to Saturday** (Lundi à Samedi)
- ✅ **Morning**: 08:30 - 13:30
- ✅ **Afternoon**: 14:30 - 17:30
- ✅ **Auto-generates**: ~18 time slots total
- ✅ **2-hour sessions** with 15-minute breaks

### What You Get:
Each day gets:
- **Séance 1**: 08:30 - 10:30 (Matinée)
- **Séance 2**: 10:45 - 12:45 (Matinée)
- **Séance 3**: 14:30 - 16:30 (Après-midi)

× 6 days = **18 time slots** automatically!

### Step 1: Go to Auto Generator
